📍 Aubrac plateau · Lozère, France — June–July 2025 My first solo road trip didn’t start solo. It started in Zurich, over a coffee and a ride with a friend, and only became solitary somewhere on the A75 heading south into the Massif Central with a week-old playlist and the particular kind of quiet that fills a car when you’re finally moving toward something. Nasbinals is small. The kind of small that barely registers on a regional map — a cluster of stone houses on the Aubrac plateau in Lozère, surrounded by open pasture and volcanic hills and air that feels ten degrees cooler than it should in late June. The fever dream of Aubrac

📍 Aubrac plateau · Lozère, France — June–July 2025 Some places you visit. Others you carry home without realising, folded into the lining of something, and only later — months later, reaching for something else entirely — do you find them there. Nasbinals is the second kind. The plateau sits above most things: above the valleys, above the treeline in places, above the noise that most of Europe runs on. The Aubrac in June is green in a way that feels almost deliberate, volcanic hills holding the horizon still, the air arriving cold from somewhere unaccounted for. You expect summer and get something older — a climate with its own logic, indifferent to the calendar. ...
