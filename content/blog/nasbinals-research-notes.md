# Nasbinals article — research notes

## The place
- Nasbinals: small town, Aubrac plateau, Lozère, southern France
- Margerie's family home — visited June–July 2025 (approx. 2 weeks), and again briefly in August
- Climate: cold even in summer, weak sun, "autumn vibes" in late August; vegetation like September/October in Slovakia
- Town fills once a year for a trail-running race weekend, then empties again
- Regular trips to Laguiole for groceries; a second family house referenced as "the other house"

## Activities & events
- Horse riding on Corrida: multiple lessons, several failed attempts, identity crisis around it ("I'm not sure anymore it's for me")
- One riding lesson that "finally worked out"
- Final morning: bareback ride on Corrida into a stream
- Trail rides out onto the Aubrac plateau
- Sunday bike ride — near-crash at 70 km/h (insect in mouth on descent), exhausting day in full sun
- Wolf park visit — described as "really nice"
- Waterfall visit with Margerie — photographs taken, "amazing"
- Dinners at L'Annexe (regular, noted as expensive but good)
- Dinner with extended family (noted as nice)

## Emotional arc in Nasbinals
- Arrived with mixed feelings: valued the place and hospitality, couldn't fully reciprocate emotionally
- Identity crisis: coffee machine breaking, failed rides, sense of things "not being in the stars"
- Gradual settling: the riding lesson that worked, the bareback ride, the last dinner
- Left with something heavy — "sad leave" recorded in journal
- Even while in Venice days later: "missing Nasbinals and Margerie"

## The rest of the trip (briefly)
- Route: Košice → Zurich (stopover with friend Rici, bike ride, pizza) → Nasbinals → Briançon → Col du Granon → Torino → Padova → Venice → Croatia
- Col du Granon: Tour de France climb above Briançon, solo ride, "very exciting"; minor mishap with torn sleeve
- Torino: evening walk; pigeon incident
- Padova: bought camera lens ("spent a lot of money"), brutal heat
- Venice: "amazing, despite me missing Nasbinals and Margerie"
- Croatia: family holiday, rest, reading (3 books), beach, bike rides, watched Tour de France; mixed feelings about crowds

## Solo travel reflection (from July 9 journal entry)
- First ever solo road trip
- "The solo travel was my first ever and it has shown me how much I enjoy it"
- Liked: personal regime, freedom, commitment, the activities
- Qualifications: "slightly repetitive," compared too much to prior year, a fight with Margerie
- Trip became a counterweight to the "I have to leave Košice" theme running through 2025

## The memory transformation (May 2026 sources)
- Nostalgia surfaced unprompted during a cottage walk, May 2026
- Triggered again when picking up tupperware from Margerie after the relationship ended
- Direct quote from journal (2026-05-17): *"My brain started to keep the Nasbinals memory as some fever dream. Everything from it feels so distant now but so beautiful. I keep a lot of it as a great memory and I can feel it will impact who I am and my future artistic endeavours. I will keep returning there and I will keep grounding myself in that place. For some reasons those two weeks were so magical that I will want to keep going back metaphysically. Maybe I will visit it one day again. For now, it must live in my memories, never to be forgotten."*
- He distinguishes nostalgia for the place from longing for Margerie specifically
